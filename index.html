Built around evidence and permission

Super Intelligence.
Not super assumptions.

Good sales intelligence needs more than an answer. It needs a source, a clear account context and an honest view of what remains unknown.

Our contact-data approach

Permitted public professional sources first. Customer-authorized records and optional customer-connected providers next. Platform-managed enrichment only after application-specific permission and commercial requirements are satisfied.

  • Source and status stay visible

    A named person is not automatically a current employee, the right buyer or a verified mailbox. Those are separate questions.

  • Customer access is not a blanket license

    Provider authentication, endpoint entitlement, permitted use and available credits must be checked independently.

  • Workspaces remain authenticated

    The application is separate from this public product website. It requires authentication; this site exposes no customer accounts, contacts or workspace data.

  • External actions need approval

    The product’s direction is guided automation with human control—not autonomous, indiscriminate outreach.

Before we talk

A few practical
questions.

Who is LeadGenSI for?

B2B sellers and teams pursuing named accounts and complex buying groups. Its initial product focus includes financial services and enterprise technology sales.

Is the platform publicly available?

LeadGenSI is in private preview. Contact us to discuss your workflow and current availability. This website is a product overview, not an open signup or the authenticated application.

Does it include a ready-made contact database?

Not by default. The approach combines permitted research and customer-authorized sources. Optional provider-backed email discovery and validation depend on permitted use, endpoint access and budget. Managed data licensing is under review.

Are Hunter or other providers approved partners?

We are evaluating provider integrations. Hunter has indicated support for customer-connected accounts under its standard terms; our managed use remains subject to product review and written consent. We do not claim a certified partnership or generally available managed enrichment.

Can it send outreach without my review?

The intended workflow keeps external outreach behind explicit approval. Research, drafts and recommendations are not the same as permission to send.
